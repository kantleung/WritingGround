# Extraordinary Politics

Project folder for a thirty-minute article on the history of shock therapy.

- `Output/Extraordinary Politics.md` — the article.
- `Output/Outline.md` — the working outline, with verified figures keyed to the draft.
- `Research/Figures.md` — the series actually used, with URLs.
- `Research/00 Source log.md` — what was fetched, what was only named, and the figures that must not be invented.
- `Research/Bolivia 1985.md` — Morales and Sachs on the New Economic Policy of 29 August 1985.
- `Research/Poland 1990.md` — Berg and Blanchard on the January programme; Balcerowicz's retrospect of 2000.
- `Research/Term ancestors and the unfetched.md` — the definition, the Chile claim, and every theoretical source the outline depends on but was not opened line by line.
