## Figures the draft must not invent

Confirmed series were not in hand for:

- The Bolivian peak inflation rate. Morales and Sachs, as fetched, say the hyperinflation ended within days of 29 August 1985. They do not, in the pages read, give the peak percentage. Do not insert 20,000 or 60,000 from memory.
- Russian GDP decline as a single percentage. Official series are commonly described as a fall on the order of two-fifths or worse to the late-1990s trough. **†** Shleifer and Treisman argue the official fall is overstated. Range only, until both series are open.
- Russian male life expectancy by year. The fall in the early 1990s, concentrated on working-age men, is not in serious dispute. The year and the decimal are. Confirm with Rosstat, WHO, or Notzon before a digit leaves conditional form.
